What Does a Professional Car Inspection Report Contain?

Published on Jan 1, 2025 • 6 min read

Buying a used car is a high-stakes decision. Relying solely on the seller's word, a quick visual glance, or a short test drive is a surefire way to invite unexpected repair bills down the road. This is where a professional pre-purchase car inspection comes in. But what exactly do you get when you pay an expert to evaluate a vehicle? At InspectCar, our comprehensive 200+ point inspection provides total transparency, summarizing the true condition of the vehicle in an easy-to-understand, highly detailed report. Let’s break down exactly what a professional car inspection report contains and how it empowers you to make a smart purchase.

The AI Health Score: An Instant Snapshot

The moment you open an InspectCar report, you are greeted with the overall AI Health Score. This score, ranging from 0 to 100, is generated using a proprietary algorithm that weights the severity of every defect found during the inspection. A score above 85 generally indicates a clean, well-maintained vehicle. A score below 60 acts as a massive red flag, indicating serious mechanical or structural issues. This score is designed to give you an immediate, high-level understanding of the car's condition before you dive into the technical details.

Category-Wise Verdicts

A vehicle is a complex machine made of thousands of components. To make the report digestible, we break down the inspection into major categories. Each category receives its own rating, allowing you to pinpoint exactly where the car's strengths and weaknesses lie:

  • Exterior & Structural: Details paint thickness gauge readings, panel gaps, rust spots, and crucial signs of structural accident repairs or frame damage.
  • Engine & Transmission: Covers oil quality, coolant state, strange noises, leaks, cold start performance, gear shift smoothness, and clutch health.
  • Interior & Electricals: Assesses the condition of the upholstery, AC performance, dashboard warning lights, power windows, infotainment system, and seat adjustments.
  • Tyres & Brakes: Provides measurements of tyre tread depth, brake pad thickness, rotor condition, and the manufacturing date of the tyres.
  • Underbody: Highlights suspension leaks, exhaust rust, damaged bushes, and fluid leaks only visible when the car is lifted.
  • Documentation: Verifies the RC, insurance validity, PUC status, service history records, and checks for loan hypothecation.

Pro Tip: Pay special attention to the Structural and Engine categories. Cosmetic interior flaws can be fixed cheaply, but a damaged frame or failing transmission will cost you a fortune.

Pass, Warn, and Fail Metrics

For every single checkpoint—over 200 of them—the report utilizes a simple traffic-light system. Pass (Green) means the component is functioning exactly as it should. Warn (Yellow) indicates that a part is showing signs of wear and will likely need replacement soon, serving as a great negotiation tool. Fail (Red) means the component is broken, unsafe, or requires immediate repair. Alongside these indicators, the inspector adds specific, plain-English notes. Instead of just marking the AC as 'Fail', the inspector's notes might specify, "AC compressor clutch is not engaging, likely requires complete replacement."

Photographic Evidence and OBD Scans

A picture is worth a thousand words, especially when dealing with car defects. A professional InspectCar report includes a comprehensive photo gallery. The inspector will photograph every scratch, dent, rust patch, and oil leak they find. Furthermore, the report contains the results of the OBD (On-Board Diagnostics) scanner. This section lists out any error codes stored in the car's computer, such as historical engine misfires or ABS sensor faults, ensuring nothing is hidden beneath the surface.

Final Recommendation and Negotiation Tips

The most valuable part of the report is the final summary. Based on all the findings, the expert mechanic will provide a definitive recommendation: Buy, Negotiate, or Avoid. If the recommendation is to negotiate, the report will arm you with a list of upcoming repair costs. You can use this exact list to bargain with the seller, often saving tens of thousands of rupees—far more than the cost of the inspection itself. The complete report is delivered digitally to your smartphone and email as a shareable PDF, allowing you to review it instantly or forward it to your mechanic for a second opinion.
