How to Check If a Used Car Has an Existing Loan Before Buying in India

Published September 25, 2026 • 8 min read

Buying a used car with an undisclosed active bank loan is one of the most common and legally dangerous mistakes Indian used car buyers make. To check if a used car has an existing loan before buying in India, inspect the physical Registration Certificate (RC) for a 'Hypothecated To' entry and verify the vehicle's registration number on the official Parivahan portal (vahan.parivahan.gov.in). InspectCar's used car inspection and document verification specialists in Bangalore routinely see buyers trapped by unpaid hypothecation, leaving them vulnerable to sudden vehicle repossession or frozen ownership transfers after handing over their hard-earned money.

What is Hypothecation on a Car RC?

Hypothecation is a legal arrangement where a borrower pledges a movable asset—in this case, a motor vehicle—as collateral to a financing institution (bank or NBFC) against an auto loan. Under Indian motor vehicle regulations, when a car is financed, the Regional Transport Office (RTO) endorses the lender's name directly onto the vehicle's Registration Certificate under the header "Hypothecated To" (HPA/HPE).

Until the loan principal and all accumulated interest are cleared in full, the bank remains the de facto primary legal owner of the vehicle. The registered person listed on the RC is legally recognized merely as the registered keeper or user, not the unencumbered absolute owner.

Why Buying a Car with Active Loan is Risky

Entering into a private used car transaction without settling or verifying an active loan exposes you to catastrophic legal and financial complications:

  • Bank Can Repossess the Car Even After You Buy It: Because the financing institution holds the primary legal lien on the chassis and engine numbers, bank recovery agents possess statutory powers under the SARFAESI Act to seize the car wherever it is parked if the previous owner defaults on their Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs). Your private sale agreement or money transfer receipt holds zero legal standing against the bank's registered charge.
  • Ownership Cannot Be Transferred Without Bank NOC: The local RTO will summarily reject Form 29 and Form 30 (ownership transfer documents) if the vehicle record reflects an active hypothecation entry. You cannot obtain an updated RC smart card in your name until the lien is formally cancelled through RTO Form 35.
  • Seller May Be in Loan Default: Desperate sellers often offer cars at lucrative "below-market" prices precisely because they are months behind on their loan payments. Once you hand over cash or NEFT payment, the seller vanishes, and recovery notices start arriving for outstanding dues that may exceed the fair market value of the car.

How to Check Hypothecation on RC Book

The first physical checkpoint is the vehicle's original Registration Certificate, whether it is a modern PVC smart card or an older laminated booklet:

  • Check the Front and Back of the Smart Card: On standard Parivahan smart cards, look for the field titled Hypothecated To or Financier / HPA. If this field displays a bank name (such as HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, or Kotak Mahindra Prime), the car was purchased with an auto loan.
  • Verify Hypothecation Cancellation Stamp: If the loan has supposedly been repaid, the RC must feature an official RTO cancellation stamp or be replaced with a fresh smart card that reads Hypothecation: NONE. Never accept a verbal claim that "the loan is closed" without supporting RTO paperwork.

How to Verify on Parivahan Portal

Physical RC cards can be forged, laminated over, or outdated if a duplicate RC was obtained under false pretenses. Therefore, digital verification on the central Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) database is mandatory:

  1. Navigate to the official Parivahan national register at vahan.parivahan.gov.in or launch the official mParivahan mobile application.
  2. Create an account or log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  3. Select Know Your Vehicle Details from the citizen services menu.
  4. Enter the vehicle's registration number (e.g., KA-01-AB-1234) and the security captcha code.
  5. Review the detailed vehicle summary screen. Scroll down to the Financier / Hypothecation row. If an institution name appears here, the national database still considers the vehicle encumbered under an active loan.

What is Form 35 (NOC) and Why You Need It?

When a borrower clears an auto loan, the bank issues a No Objection Certificate (NOC) along with two signed physical copies of Form 35 (Notice of Termination of an Agreement of Hire-Purchase/Lease/Hypothecation). This document is addressed to the jurisdictional Registering Authority and certifies that the bank has received full settlement and has no objection to removing their lien from the RC.

Bank NOC letters generally carry an expiration window of 90 days from the date of issue. If the seller has held the NOC for longer than three months without submitting Form 35 to the RTO to strike off the hypothecation, the document lapses and the bank must issue a revalidated NOC.

Steps to Ensure Safe Purchase of Loan-Encumbered Car

If you have identified a car you genuinely wish to purchase but discover an existing loan, follow this strict four-step protocol to prevent financial catastrophe:

  1. Demand Form 35 (NOC) From the Lender Before Paying Any Money: Insist that the seller present original Form 35 copies bearing original bank seals, an unexpired bank NOC letter, and a loan closure statement showing zero balance outstanding.
  2. Verify NOC Directly With the Financing Bank: Fraudulent NOC printouts on forged bank letterheads are a recognized racket in major cities. Visit or call the issuing branch manager directly to verify the loan account closure number.
  3. Track Parivahan Database Updates After RTO Submission: Ensure Form 35, Form 29, Form 30, and the original RC are lodged at the RTO for simultaneous hypothecation cancellation and ownership transfer.
  4. Complete Final Financial Settlement Only When Clear: Retain a substantial token balance (at least 20% to 30% of the vehicle value) or deposit the funds in escrow, releasing the final payment only when the Parivahan portal confirms hypothecation is removed.

Frequently Asked Questions About Car Loans and NOC

What is hypothecation on a car RC?

Hypothecation is a legal endorsement on the vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) indicating that a bank or NBFC holds a financial lien over the car as collateral until the auto loan is fully settled.

How do I check if a used car has a loan?

Check the physical RC book or smart card for a 'Hypothecated To' entry, and cross-verify the vehicle registration number on the official Parivahan portal (vahan.parivahan.gov.in) under vehicle status.

What is Form 35 for a car?

Form 35 is the statutory RTO notice and No Objection Certificate (NOC) issued in duplicate by the lender confirming that the car loan has been fully closed and hypothecation should be cancelled.

Can a car with active loan be transferred to my name?

No, the Regional Transport Office (RTO) will strictly reject any ownership transfer application while an active hypothecation lien remains recorded against the vehicle registration.
