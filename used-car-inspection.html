Exterior & Body Inspection Details

Our technicians methodically inspect every exterior panel, seam, and structural member. We look for irregular panel gaps around the hood, trunk, and doors which invariably indicate prior collision repairs. Using digital paint thickness gauges that measure down to a single micron, we test fenders, quarter panels, doors, and the roof. While original factory paint typically reads between 80 and 130 microns, panels that have undergone bodywork and respraying show elevated readings of 200 to 500+ microns. We also thoroughly check A, B, and C pillars for spot-weld integrity to guarantee the passenger safety cell has never been compromised.

Engine & Mechanical Health Evaluation

The powertrain is the most expensive assembly to repair on any motor vehicle. InspectCar assesses engine oil condition for metallic particles, sludge formation, and fuel dilution. We test the cooling system for hydrocarbon contamination (a classic sign of a blown cylinder head gasket) and examine drive belts, tensioners, engine mounts, and alternator performance. In our test drive, we analyze clutch bite height, gearbox synchromesh engagement, torque converter lockup, or dual-clutch transmission hesitation under load.

OBD-II Electronic Diagnostic Scanning

Modern vehicles contain dozens of microprocessors monitoring everything from fuel injection timing to anti-lock braking sensors. Shrewd sellers often clear dashboard warning lights just before a prospective buyer arrives. Our commercial-grade OBD-II diagnostic scanner reads deeper into the vehicle ECU, pulling stored Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), historical fault logs, emission readiness statuses, and live sensor readings. If a check-engine warning was cleared 15 kilometers ago, our diagnostic system immediately identifies the event.

Interior, Flood Checks & Electrical Components

Bangalore suffers from severe localized flooding during monsoon seasons, leading to thousands of submerged vehicles being cosmetically cleaned and quietly offered for resale. InspectCar conducts dedicated flood checks: we pull back footwell carpets to inspect sound-deadening felt for silt, look for rust on unpainted seat brackets, examine the fuse box for water residue, and test harness connectors. We also verify the operation of every switch, power window, airbag warning circuit, and cabin air conditioning cooling performance.

Tyres, Brakes & Underbody Structural Chassis

A vehicle's underside tells the truest story of its operating history. Our inspectors check the exhaust system for rust and catalytic converter removal, look for oil leaks running along the transmission tunnel, and examine the suspension control arms, tie rod ends, and shock absorbers. We inspect all four tyres plus the spare wheel, measuring tread depth and verifying the four-digit DOT date code to guarantee the tyres are not dangerously old, dry-rotted, or unevenly worn due to chassis misalignment.

Vehicle Documentation & Odometer Fraud Analysis

A mechanically sound vehicle with problematic paperwork can turn into a legal and financial nightmare. We verify the Registration Certificate (RC), engine number, and chassis VIN number against the physical stampings on the vehicle's firewall. We check for outstanding hypothecation loans, review past insurance claims (NCB percentage), check PUC validity, and cross-reference the physical odometer reading with available dealership service records and wear patterns on driver touchpoints.

Common Issues We Find in Bangalore's Used Car Market

Our certified inspectors examine hundreds of vehicles across Bangalore every month. Here are the most prevalent undisclosed issues uncovered during our pre-purchase inspections:

Repainted Accident Panels (35%+ of Cars)

By far the most common issue. Fenders, doors, and hoods repainted with thick putty to cover major dents, rust, or structural accident impacts.

Odometer Rollback & Mileage Fraud

Digital odometers tampered down by 40,000 to 80,000 kilometers to demand an inflated market price while disguising overdue major maintenance.

Concealed Monsoon Flood Damage

Submerged cars during heavy rains in Bangalore that have been cleaned cosmetically but suffer from corroded wiring, mold, and dying ECUs.

Cleared OBD Error Codes & Engine Misfires

Check engine warning lights reset just prior to inspection to hide costly catalytic converter failures, faulty ABS sensors, or cylinder misfires.

Rodent Damage to Wiring Looms

Chewed engine wiring harnesses and sensor wires caused by rats in apartment basement parking, creating intermittent and expensive electrical faults.

Worn Clutch & Transmission Shudder

Severe clutch wear and dual-clutch transmission (DCT/DSG) overheating caused by Bangalore's grinding bumper-to-bumper city traffic.
