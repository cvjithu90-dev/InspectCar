Why New Car PDI is Essential in Bangalore — Even for Brand New Cars

Most car buyers assume a brand new car fresh from the showroom is perfect. This assumption is dangerously wrong. Vehicles travel over 1,000 km from the manufacturing plant — Maruti from Manesar, Hyundai from Chennai, Toyota from Bidadi — often in open car carriers exposed to weather, road debris, and handling damage. They then sit in dealership stockyards for weeks or months.

In Bangalore's tropical climate, stockyard storage comes with real risks: rat bites damage wiring harnesses, moisture causes interior mold, UV exposure fades plastic trim, and battery drain affects ECU calibration. InspectCar has found significant defects — including repainted panels and electrical faults — in over 25% of new cars presented for PDI at Bangalore dealerships.

An independent pre-delivery inspection at ₹1,999 is the smartest ₹2,000 you'll spend on a car that costs ₹8–50 lakh.

What InspectCar's New Car PDI Covers — 300+ Point Check

Our certified automotive inspectors deploy calibrated digital tools to conduct an uncompromising audit of your new vehicle across six vital domains:

Paint & Body Panels

Using a professional paint thickness gauge, our inspector measures paint depth on every panel — hood, roof, all four doors, boot, front and rear bumpers. Factory paint is uniform at 90–130 microns. Repainted panels show higher readings (160–300+ microns), indicating transit damage repair that the dealer may not disclose.

Glass & Markings

Every glass pane is checked for the OEM etching, manufacturing date, and any chips, cracks, or distortions. Replacement glass (due to transit damage) may lack OEM markings — a major quality compromise that InspectCar's PDI flags.

Electrical Systems & Infotainment

All 50+ electrical functions are tested: infotainment touchscreen, reverse camera, Bluetooth, USB, all interior lights, all window regulators, central locking, sunroof (if applicable), horn, all exterior lights, indicators, and ADAS features. We also run an OBD scan to check for any pre-registered DTCs (error codes) that may indicate prior issues.

Air Conditioning

AC cooling performance is tested at idle and high RPM. Compressor engagement, temperature drop rate, and all vent modes are verified. Poor cooling in a brand new car may indicate a refrigerant leak or compressor issue.

Tyres & Underbody

All 4 tyres are checked for tread depth (should be 8+ mm), OEM brand, size matching, and DOT manufacturing week/year code. The spare tyre is verified for size and pressure. Underbody is inspected for transit damage, fluid leaks, and any impact marks.

Documentation & Accessories Check

We verify: Form 21, Form 22, manufacturer invoice, warranty booklet, owner's manual, 2 sets of keys, spare tyre with tool kit, and every accessory listed on your booking invoice. Missing accessories at delivery are common — dealers sometimes substitute or omit items. Our report documents everything so you can demand compliance before signing the delivery gate pass.

Bangalore's New Car Delivery — Common Defects We Find

Bangalore is home to some of the highest car delivery volumes in India. Due to high stockyard turnover and prolonged open-air storage along peri-urban highway belts, InspectCar PDI inspectors regularly uncover defects that showroom sales executives fail to disclose.

Real Defects Discovered During Dealership PDIs in Bangalore:

  • Paint touch-ups on hoods or boot lids: Common consequence of stone chips and ratchet straps during multi-tier trailer transit.
  • Single repainted door or fender: Often caused by minor stockyard shunting collisions or maneuvering scrapes in cramped dealer compounds.
  • Rat-bitten wiring in the engine bay harness: Particularly frequent in vehicles stored in open mud stockyards in Nelamangala, Bommasandra, or Hosur Road.
  • Demo car odometer readings above 200 km: Vehicles driven between showroom branches or utilized as unauthorized customer test drives passed off as fresh stock.
  • Wrong accessories delivered: Cheaper tyre brands substituted, or omission of promised mudguards, matting, or boot organizers listed on the invoice.
  • OBD diagnostic codes pre-existing: Historical DTC fault codes stored from dead battery jumps, alternator voltage drops, or sensor calibration issues during shipping.
  • Missing second key or missing documentation stamps: Lack of authorized dealership stamps on the initial pre-delivery maintenance schedule booklet.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Car PDI in Bangalore

Clear, authoritative answers to help you navigate dealership delivery with total confidence

1. What is PDI (Pre-Delivery Inspection) for a new car?

PDI is an independent check performed on a brand new car at the dealership before you take delivery. It ensures there are no manufacturing defects, transit damage, or missing accessories — protecting your investment from day one.

2. Is PDI mandatory before taking delivery of a new car in India?

PDI is not legally mandatory but is strongly recommended by automotive experts. New cars regularly arrive with transit damage, repainted panels, rat-bitten wiring, or missing accessories. An independent PDI by InspectCar ensures you receive exactly what you paid for.

3. How much does new car PDI cost in Bangalore?

InspectCar's new car Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) costs ₹1,999 (Pre-Drive package) covering 300+ checkpoints including paint gauge, OBD scan, all electrical systems, documentation check, and accessory verification.

4. How long does a new car PDI take?

A new car PDI by InspectCar typically takes 45 to 75 minutes at the dealership or stockyard in Bangalore.

5. What does InspectCar check during new car PDI?

InspectCar checks body panel alignment, paint thickness (gauge test), glass markings, all electrical systems, AC performance, OBD scan for any pre-registered DTCs, fluid levels, tyre condition and DOT code, documentation (Form 21, Form 22, invoice), and all accessories listed on the invoice.

6. Can a brand new car have paint defects?

Yes. Factory paint runs, touch-ups, and swirl marks from dealership washing are common. Transit accidents can cause panel repainting. A paint gauge test detects repainted panels even when they look perfect to the naked eye.

7. What is stockyard damage and how common is it?

Stockyard damage occurs when cars are stored in open lots for weeks or months before delivery. It includes rat bite wiring, battery drain, hail damage, UV fading, and moisture ingress. InspectCar has found stockyard damage in over 20% of new car PDIs in Bangalore.

8. When should I book a new car PDI — before or after registration?

Always book a PDI BEFORE registration. Once you sign the delivery gate pass and register the car, any defects become your responsibility. Book InspectCar's PDI after the dealer allocates a specific VIN/chassis number to you.

9. What happens if InspectCar finds defects during new car PDI?

If defects are found, InspectCar's detailed report gives you written evidence to demand the dealer rectify all issues before delivery, request a replacement vehicle, or negotiate complimentary accessories as compensation.

10. Can I reject a new car if the PDI fails?

Yes. If significant defects are found before registration and delivery gate pass signing, you have the right to demand repairs or a replacement vehicle. InspectCar's report provides the documented proof you need.

11. Does InspectCar do PDI at the dealership in Bangalore?

Yes, InspectCar inspectors visit the dealership or stockyard anywhere in Bangalore at your scheduled time. We cover all major automotive dealership hubs including Tumkur Road, Old Madras Road, Hosur Road, Bellary Road, and Mysore Road dealer corridors.

12. What documents should I check at new car delivery?

Check: Form 21 (Notice of dispatch), Form 22 (Roadworthiness certificate), manufacturer invoice, warranty booklet, owner's manual, insurance policy, 2 sets of keys, spare tyre with jack and wheel spanner, and all accessories listed on your booking invoice.

13. How can I tell if a new car was previously used as a demo car?

Signs of a demo car include odometer reading above 200 km, wear on accelerator pedal rubber, scuffs on door sill plates, service history entry before your purchase date, and fuel level below 50%. InspectCar's PDI specifically checks for all these indicators.

14. Does InspectCar's PDI check for rat bite wiring in new cars?

Yes. Rat bite damage to wiring harnesses is a common issue in cars stored in dealership stockyards in Bangalore. Our inspectors check engine bay wiring, underbody harnesses, and run a full electrical system test to detect this issue before delivery.

15. Can I use InspectCar's PDI report to get dealer value-additions?

Yes. If our PDI report documents any issues — even minor ones — it gives you strong negotiating leverage with the dealer to request complimentary accessories (3D mats, mud flaps, dashcam), price match on insurance, or extended warranty coverage.
